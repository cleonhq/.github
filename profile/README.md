### 🚀 About Me

I am the **Chief Operating Officer (COO)** at **Cleon Joint Stock Company (Cleon JSC)**, a Vietnam-based digital agency and technology solutions provider established in 2018. 

With a strong focus on operational excellence and digital ecosystems, I oversee the strategy, service delivery, and infrastructure that empower businesses to build, manage, and scale their online presence seamlessly—avoiding fragmentation through centralized, all-in-one digital asset management.

- 🏢 **Current Role:** Chief Operating Officer @ [Cleon JSC](https://cleon.vn) (Hanoi, Vietnam)
- 🎯 **Core Focus:** Business Operations, Digital Marketing Strategy, Web Architecture & Cloud Hosting Solutions
- 💡 **Philosophy:** *"Simplicity in design, efficiency in operations, and measurable growth in digital execution."*

---

### 🛠️ What We Build & Deliver at Cleon JSC

At **Cleon**, we help SMEs and enterprises accelerate their digital transformation through four core pillars:

- 🌐 **Custom Web Design & Development:** Mobile-first, SEO-optimized, and W3C-standardized websites built for high performance and conversion.
- ⚙️ **Web Administration & Asset Management:** Centralized maintenance, security hardening, and continuous optimization so clients can focus purely on their core business.
- ☁️ **High-Speed Cloud Hosting & VPS:** Dedicated cloud infrastructure (SSL-secured, high-uptime, NVMe/SSD-powered) tailored specifically for high-traffic and ad-driven platforms.
- 📈 **Performance Digital Marketing:** Multi-channel digital advertising and growth strategies backed by data and technical SEO.
